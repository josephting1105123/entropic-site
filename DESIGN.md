# Design System: Entropic Software Labs

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Each product in its own room"**

The site is a plain shell (a sticky top bar, a footer, light and dark tokens in `css/site.css`) with product pages that each commit to one physical setting and draw everything from it: the ground, the inks, the type, the components. A world page sets its own `color-scheme`, overrides the shell tokens or scopes its rules under `body[data-product="..."]`, and ignores the visitor's light/dark choice. Three pages do this now: Spectrum+ (a photocopied handout), Card Games (a Chinese New Year open-house card table) and Quick Vote (the app's own light theme). The worlds don't share inks, faces or components with each other.

Everything ships under a `default-src 'self'` CSP. There are no inline styles or scripts, fonts are self-hosted WOFF2 under `/assets/fonts/`, textures are SVG files under `/assets/textures/`, and interaction is CSS where it can be (Card Games' seat view is pure `:has()` on radios). Per-element sizes that JS has to set go through the CSSOM, never a `style=""` attribute.

**Key Characteristics:**
- A shared shell, plus one world per product page, each scoped to `body[data-product]`.
- Grounds are real materials drawn in CSS and SVG: toner noise on grey paper, floral vinyl, and the Quick Vote app's pale slate ground.
- Each world has one self-hosted face for display. Spectrum+ also sets body text in it (Archivo); Quick Vote uses the app's Noto Serif and Geist pair; Card Games uses system sans for body.
- Motion plays once on load, is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and the static end state is the default.
- The real app always appears: as a screenshot in a CSS phone, or as a working replica.

### Shared shell

- **Tokens.** Dark is the default (`--bg` #141414, `--card` #1F1F1F, `--text` #F5F5F5, `--accent` #22C55E). Light applies under `prefers-color-scheme: light` unless `data-theme="dark"`, or under `data-theme="light"` (`--bg` #F8FAFC, `--text` #0F172A, `--accent-text` #047857). Radii are `--r-sm` 8px, `--r-md` 12px, `--r-lg` 16px and `--r-pill` 999px. `--gutter` is clamp(16px, 5vw, 48px) and `--section` is clamp(4rem, 3rem + 5vw, 7rem).
- **Type.** System sans (`--sans`) for UI, Noto Serif (`--serif`) for shell headings.
- **Per-product accents.** `site.css` sets `--accent`, `--accent-text`, `--accent-on` and `--accent-muted` per `[data-product]`, in both themes. They tint the shell's selection, links, focus ring and drawer state.
- **Top bar.** `.topbar` is sticky, 64px tall, on `var(--bg)` with a hairline bottom border. World pages keep it and recolour it. Spectrum+ puts it on Sheet with a 2px toner rule. Card Games turns it into a Stool Red strip with cream type. Quick Vote overrides the shell tokens on its body with the app's light palette, so the bar stays light in either OS theme. Card Games and Quick Vote hide the theme toggle.
- **Footer.** `.footer` has a hairline top border and `--text-3` at 0.9rem. World pages may restyle it in their own inks (Card Games puts it on a solid vinyl strip).

**The Own World Rule.** A world page pins its `color-scheme`, scopes every rule to `body[data-product="..."]` or a page prefix (`sp-`, `cg-`, `qv-`), and leaves `site.css` alone. The only shared parts are the top bar, the Projects drawer and the footer, and a world may restyle them in its own inks.

## Spectrum+: the photocopied handout

The existing precedent (`css/spectrum.css`, `js/spectrum.js`).

**Thesis.** The page is a photocopied class handout that someone went over with highlighters.

### Colors
- **Copier Grey** (#e3e3e0, `--paper`): the page ground. **Darker Grey** (#d5d5d1) is the scrollbar track. **Sheet** (#ededeb) is the lifted handout sheets.
- **Toner** (#1f1e1c): all text, 2px rules and ticket borders. **Faded Toner** (#474744): fine print, table heads, metadata.
- **Highlighter Yellow** (#f4ff3a): "yours". It marks highlights, download tickets, selection and the checked occupancy button.
- **Highlighter Pink** (#ff5aa8): "changed". It marks highlights, the focus ring and link hover.
- **Highlighter Green** (#3df28f): "due".

**The Highlighter Means Something Rule.** The three highlighters are a legend, not decoration: yellow is yours, pink is changed, green is due. Everything else is toner on grey.

### Typography
Archivo, self-hosted as a variable font (weight 400–900, width 62–125%) with a latin and a latin-ext subset. Headings use the condensed cut (`font-stretch: 66%`, letter-spacing -0.02em, balanced wrap). The H1 and the closing title are clamp(3rem, 9vw, 6rem) at 890, line-height 0.9. Section titles are clamp(2.25rem, 6vw, 3.75rem) at 860, with a max width of 18ch. Panel and widget titles are 1.9rem at 860. Body text is 1.0625rem at 400, line-height 1.55, paragraphs capped at 65ch, with `strong` at 650. Fine print is 0.9rem in Faded Toner.

### Materials and ground
Grey paper, with a fixed full-page SVG `feTurbulence` noise layer at 18% opacity in `multiply` (the toner grain). Sheets carry a radial vignette and sit slightly rotated (−0.4° to −0.8° on the hero sheet, ±0.4° on the panels). Corners are uneven on purpose (`2px 3px 2px 4px`). Highlighter strokes are `linear-gradient` backgrounds at 82% height in `multiply`, cloned across line breaks. The comparison column uses a 6px halftone dot. Rules are dashed toner at 40%.

### Signature components
- `.hl`, `.hl--changed`, `.hl--due`, `.row-title`: the highlighter stroke.
- `.sheet` / `.hero-sheet`: the handout sheet.
- `.ticket`: a download ticket. Yellow fill, 2px toner border, irregular radius, 48px minimum height. It turns dashed when disabled.
- `.stamp`: a rotated rubber stamp (−6°) through the `#ink` SVG filter.
- `.tt` / `.cls`: the timetable, with moved classes struck through.
- `.widget-card`: the home-screen widget, with a 2px border and 24–30px uneven radii.

### Motion
Highlighter strokes sweep in from 0% to 100% width over 520ms on `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`. Tickets and occupancy buttons move 1px over 150ms. This page predates the gating convention: it disables transitions under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` instead of opting in under `no-preference`.

### Extending it
Use the toner-on-grey palette and add colour only through the three highlighter meanings. New panels are `.sheet`s with uneven corners and a small rotation. Never add a fourth highlighter colour, and never fill a whole area with a highlighter.

## Card Games: the CNY open-house table

`card-games/index.html`, `css/card-games.css`, `assets/textures/tablecloth.svg`, `assets/fonts/bricolage-latin.woff2`. There is no page script of its own; desktop snapping comes from the shared `css/segments.css` and `js/segments.js`.

**Thesis.** The page is the family card table at a Chinese New Year open house, seen from above: floral vinyl cloth, real playing cards, a phone with the app, mandarin oranges and a score pad.

### Colors
Light only (`color-scheme: light`). Reds and graphite on cream card stock, with one green.

#### Primary
- **Stool Red** (#C8102E, `--cg-stool`): the top bar, the drawer head, the drawer's selected view pill and "all projects" button, `theme-color`, the Play button fill and border, the checked seat chip, the bullets in the facts list, the caret and the scrollbar thumb. On press and hover the buttons darken to **Pressed Red** (#A50D25), which is also the text colour of the outline button.
- **Ink Red** (#9E1328, `--cg-deep`): H1, H2, the step numerals on the score pad, the eye-icon stroke.
- **Cloth Red** (#D9283C, `--cg-red`): the score pad's binding strip and the peonies in the tablecloth.
- **Card Back Red** (#B71C2C): face-down cards, with a white diagonal lattice and a 3px white inset border.

#### Secondary
- **Leaf Green** (#2E8B57, `--cg-leaf`): the focus ring (3px solid) everywhere on the page and in the drawer, except inside the red top bar and drawer head, where the ring is Card Stock cream. Also the leaves in the cloth.
- **Deep Leaf** (#236C44, `--cg-leaf-ink`): text links in the status line, darkening to #174D31 on hover.

#### Neutral
- **Vinyl White** (#F4F1EA): the page ground under the texture, the footer strip and the scrollbar track.
- **Card Stock** (#FFFDF8): placards, seat tags and the 10px table rim.
- **Pad Paper** (#FFFEF9): score-pad sheets, ruled in **Pad Blue** (#BFD3E6) every 1.75rem with a 2px **Margin Pink** (#E9A3AC) margin line at 2.5rem.
- **Card Edge** (#E3DCCF): 1px borders on placards and seat-note dividers, and the unchecked seat-chip border. Playing-card faces are #FFFFFF with a #DDD5C8 edge.
- **Pencil Graphite** (#2B2622): all body text and the footer text.
- **Pip Black** (#1D1B19): black suits. Red suits use Stool Red.
- **Legend Brown** (#5A4A40): the "Look from" legend.

Selection is #FBD3D9 on graphite.

**The Paper on Cloth Rule.** Text never sits directly on the tablecloth. Every heading, paragraph and button is on card stock: a `.cg-placard`, a `.cg-sheet`, a `.cg-tag` or the footer strip. Only objects (the phone, cards, oranges, the table) sit on the cloth itself.

### Typography
**Display font:** Bricolage Grotesque, self-hosted variable (weight 200–800, width 75–100%), always at width 100%, with a system-ui fallback.
**Body font:** system sans (`system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif`).

- **Display:** H1 at 800, clamp(2.6rem, 8vw, 6rem), line-height 0.9, letter-spacing −0.02em, in Ink Red.
- **Headline:** H2 at 800, clamp(1.9rem, 4vw, 2.6rem), line-height 1. The closing H2 is clamp(2rem, 5vw, 2.6rem). On score pads the H2 is 2rem with a 3.5rem line-height, so it takes two ruled lines.
- **Title:** step H3s at 700, 1.15rem, on the 1.75rem ruling.
- **Body:** 1.0625rem, line-height 1.6, 46ch max on placards and 40ch in the close. On the pads, all text locks to a 1.75rem line-height so it sits on the blue rules.
- **Buttons:** Bricolage at 700, 1.0625rem.
- **Labels:** the status line ("Web · Live · Open source") is 600 at 0.9rem. The seat legend is 700 at 0.8rem, uppercase, tracked 0.08em. Seat tags are 700 at 0.72rem (0.8rem from 700px).
- **Step numerals:** CSS counters in Bricolage 800 at 2.25rem, set in the pad's left margin.
- **Code:** a system monospace at 0.9em on #F1ECE2.

**The Ruled Line Rule.** Everything on a score pad keeps the 1.75rem line pitch, including headings (two lines) and step gaps (one line). New pad content must land on the rules.

### Materials and ground
- **Tablecloth.** `assets/textures/tablecloth.svg` tiles at 360px over Vinyl White: red (#D9283C, #E8576A) and pink (#F4A3AE) peonies with green leaves (#2E8B57, #3FA36B) on #F6F3EC. A fixed 115° white sheen gradient on top makes the vinyl look glossy.
- **Card stock.** Placards are 14px radius with a 1px Card Edge border and the paper shadow.
- **Score pad.** Pad Paper with blue rules, a pink margin line, a 14px Cloth Red binding strip across the top, a 3px radius, and a tilt from 700px (−1.2° for steps, +0.8° for facts).
- **Phone.** A #161412 body with a 30px radius, a 12px bezel and a side button and camera bump drawn in pseudo-elements. It holds the real screenshot `card-games-table.webp` at an 18px radius and lies at −2° (−4° from 900px).
- **Cards.** Built from markup with an inline SVG suit sprite (`#s-heart`, `#s-diamond`, `#s-club`, `#s-spade`). Corners carry rank and suit, with a centre pip. The joker has a vertical "JOKER" and an outlined star.
- **Oranges.** An inline SVG of two mandarins with a soft drop shadow, at the stage's lower right.
- **Round table.** A 560px max circle of tablecloth with a 10px Card Stock rim and an inset shadow.

### Signature components
- `.cg-placard`: a card-stock panel. It holds the hero (`.cg-hero-card`), the seat view (`.cg-fair-panel`) and the close (`.cg-close-card`).
- `.btn-play` / `.btn-src`: a red Play button and a white outline button, both 48px minimum height with a 2px Stool Red border and a 12px radius. They lift 1px on hover and drop 1px on press.
- `.cg-stage` > `.cg-phone` + `.cg-hand` + `.cg-oranges`: the angled phone, five fanned cards (`.cg-pcard`, ±9° and ±18°, pivoting from the bottom centre) overlapping its lower-left edge, and the oranges.
- `.cg-seats` + `.cg-table`: the "Look from" seat view. Three visually hidden radios with pill labels (`.cg-seats label`, 44px minimum height). `.cg-fair:has(#seat-…:checked)` flips one seat's fan face up (`rotateY(180deg)` on `.cg-card`, with `backface-visibility: hidden` faces) and swaps the visible `.cg-seat-note`. The notes stack in one grid cell, so the panel never changes height. Without `:has()` support the radios are hidden and the landlord view stays.
- `.cg-tag`: a pill label on card stock ("17 cards", "On the table").
- `.cg-sheet` with `.cg-steps` (numbered) and `.cg-facts` (red dot bullets): the score pads.

### Shell and snapping
- **Snapping.** On desktop (1000px and up, with hover and a fine pointer) `js/segments.js` adds `html.is-segmented`, and the four sections (hero, fair, pad, close) snap one per screen, each centred in its own grid. `data-rail="off"` turns off the chapter rail. Touch and narrow screens scroll normally.
- **Shell tokens.** The page redefines the shell tokens on `:root:root:root body[data-product="card-games"]`: `--bg` and `--card` Card Stock, `--surface` Vinyl White, `--text` Pencil Graphite, `--text-2`/`--text-3` Legend Brown, `--hairline` Card Edge, `--accent` Stool Red, `--accent-text` Ink Red, `--accent-on` Card Stock. `theme-color` is Stool Red.
- **Top bar.** A Stool Red strip with no border and a soft red-brown shadow. The brand text is Card Stock in Bricolage 700, the mark sits on a Card Stock tile (6px radius) in red, and Projects is a cream outline pill (1.5px at 60%, a 14% cream wash on hover) in Bricolage 700. The theme toggle is hidden.
- **Projects drawer.** A Card Stock sheet with a Stool Red head (the title in Bricolage 800 at 1.6rem in Card Stock, a cream outline close pill). The view switch is a Vinyl White pill track with a Stool Red selected pill. Group headings are Ink Red Bricolage 800. Links are graphite with a Vinyl White hover and an 8% red wash on the current page. Source badges are red outlines (dashed Legend Brown when closed). "All projects" is a 48px Stool Red pill in Bricolage 700 that darkens to Pressed Red.
- **Focus.** Leaf Green everywhere, Card Stock cream inside the red top bar and drawer head (2px offset in both).

### Motion
Everything sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, and the static layout is the end state.
- **Deal:** each hero card drops from 60px above, rotated 20° further, over 700ms on `--cg-ease` (`cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`). Delays run 150ms + 70ms per card, with `backwards` fill. It plays once on load.
- **Seat flip:** fan cards turn over in 450ms on `--cg-ease`, staggered 30ms per card.
- **Controls:** buttons and seat chips change colour and position over 160ms.

### Extending it
- **The Play Money Rule.** Coins are play money only. Never show real currency, cash, payment or prize imagery. New copy and visuals must not add betting or winnings language; the existing facts name in-game tables and stakes and say plainly that there are no real-money stakes.
- **The Real Card Rule.** Cards are white stock with red and black pips, built in markup with the suit sprite. Never use glyph suits or images. Card backs are Card Back Red with the white lattice.
- Text goes on card stock (the Paper on Cloth Rule). Facts go on a ruled pad.
- Any new interaction is CSS-first: radios, `:has()` and an `@supports not selector(:has(*))` fallback.
- Add new materials only from the same table (tea cups, red packets, a cloth corner), drawn in SVG or CSS and served from `/assets`.

## Quick Vote: the app's own light theme

### Motion
The animations sit inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, and the marked ballot is the static state.
- **Ballot:** rises 12px and fades in over 500ms on `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`.

- **Live dot:** fades to 35% and back on a 2.4s loop.
- **Buttons:** the primary button lifts 1px on hover over 150ms.

### Extending it
- **The Host Gate Rule.** The selling point is that only people the host lets in can vote. Any new hero or illustration keeps the gate visible: the PIN, the voter roll, and at least one name still waiting. Don't lead with results, charts or speed.
- Take new colours from the app's light tokens, never invent page-only ones. If the app's palette changes, change this page with it.
- Keep the demo PIN 482 915 consistent between the ballot and the live phone.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** scope every world rule under `body[data-product="..."]` or the page prefix, and pin `color-scheme` on world pages.
- **Do** self-host every face as WOFF2 with `font-display: swap`, and draw textures as SVG files in `/assets/textures/`. The CSP allows nothing inline.
- **Do** gate every animation and transition under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` and make the static layout the finished state.
- **Do** show the real product, as a screenshot in a CSS phone (Card Games) or a working replica (Quick Vote).
- **Do** keep focus rings at 3px in a colour from the world: Leaf Green (#2E8B57) on Card Games (Card Stock cream inside its red top bar and drawer head), Deep Emerald (#047857) on Quick Vote, Highlighter Pink (#ff5aa8) on Spectrum+.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px minimum (48px for primary buttons).

### Don't:
- **Don't** imply gambling on Card Games. Coins are play money only: no currency symbols, cash, payouts or casino imagery.
- **Don't** give Card Games a green-felt page ground. Felt appears only inside the app screenshot.
- **Don't** lead Quick Vote with anything but the host gate, and don't style it as a white polling page with a purple accent.
- **Don't** borrow one world's ink, face or component in another world.
- **Don't** put text straight on the Card Games tablecloth.
- **Don't** add inline `style=""` or `<script>` blocks. Set runtime sizes through the CSSOM, as `js/qv-phone.js` does.
