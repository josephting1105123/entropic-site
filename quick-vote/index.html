Quick Vote

Web Live

Make a poll, read out the PIN, and everyone votes from their phone. The host approves who gets in and decides when people see the results.

Open Quick Vote Source on GitHub

How it works

  1. Make a poll

    Write a question, add 2 to 20 options, and choose whether people pick one or several. Turn on Anonymous to hide names.

  2. Share the PIN

    Voters open the site, type the 6-digit PIN and their name, and wait for you to let them in.

  3. Show the results

    Results stay hidden until you switch Live on. Then everyone's screen shows them at once.

On a voter's phone

Details

  • No accounts and nothing to install.
  • Single or multiple choice, with names shown to the host or fully anonymous.
  • Results show as bars that update as each vote lands, with the leading option in green.
  • Load-tested with 300 people voting at the same moment.
