Entropy means randomness, and that's the idea behind the name. Our apps don't stick to one sector. What they share is the approach: find one thing that's frustrating, then build the app that handles it cleanly.

How we build

  • One problem each

    Every app starts from one specific annoyance, and we keep it focused on that instead of growing into a suite.

  • Fix the design, not just the feature

    Sometimes the tool already exists and is just awkward to use. Then the job is the layout and the flow, not another feature.

  • No account

    Every app opens straight away. None of them asks you to sign up.

Questions

What is Entropic Software Labs?

A small software studio. We build apps for Android, Windows and the web, and we don't stick to one sector: file transfer, polls, calendars, card games and church bookkeeping so far.

Do I need an account to use your apps?

No. None of our apps asks you to sign up.

Is your code open source?

Most of it. Projects marked Open source on the projects page have their code on GitHub. A few are closed source.

Which platforms do you support?

Android, Windows and the web. Each project's page says which ones it runs on.

Can I suggest an app or report a bug?

Yes. Email us at the address below. For open-source projects you can also open an issue on GitHub.
