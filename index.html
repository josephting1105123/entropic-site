Entropic Software Labs makes small apps that each do one job. They work without an account, and wherever we can, your data stays on your own devices.

How we build

  • No sign-up

    OffBridge and Quick Vote work without an account. Open them and start.

  • Device to device

    OffBridge sends straight between your phone and PC, sealed with AES-256-GCM. Nothing goes through a server.

  • You confirm first

    Schedule adds nothing to your calendar until you say so. In Quick Vote, the host decides who gets in and when results show.

The apps

OffBridge

Android Windows Free

Send files and sync your clipboard between an Android phone and a Windows PC, device to device. No router, no internet and no account needed.
