We build apps for whatever problem gets our attention: moving files, running polls, reading calendars, playing cards, keeping a church's books. Each one fixes something that was missing, or redesigns something that was hard to use.

See the apps GitHub

Entropy means randomness, and that's the idea behind the name. Our apps don't stick to one sector. What they share is the approach: find one thing that's frustrating, then build the app that handles it cleanly.

How we build

  • One problem each

    Every app starts from one specific annoyance. OffBridge moves files between phone and PC without the cloud. Quick Vote polls a room without anyone signing up.

  • Fix the design, not just the feature

    Sometimes the tool already exists and is just awkward to use. Schedule reads the calendars you already have and lays them out properly. Card Games puts classic games on a table that fits a phone screen.

  • No account

    Every app opens straight away. None of them asks you to sign up.
