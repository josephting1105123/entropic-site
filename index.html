OffBridge

Android Windows Free

Send files and sync your clipboard between an Android phone and a Windows PC, device to device. No router, no internet and no account needed.

  • Pair once with a QR code or a 6-digit code that both screens must match.
  • Sends over your own Wi-Fi, or with no network at all through QR, NFC, Wi-Fi Direct or a hotspot.
  • Everything is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it leaves the device. Nothing goes through a server.

Quick Vote

Web Live

Make a poll, read out the PIN, and everyone votes from their phone. The host approves who gets in and decides when people see the results.

  • No accounts and nothing to install.
  • Single or multiple choice, with names shown to the host or fully anonymous.
  • Load-tested with 300 people voting at the same moment.

Android In development
