MIF Finance

Windows Android Closed source

A Windows app and an Android companion for a church treasurer. The Windows app holds the weekly offerings, the expense claims, the budget and the year-end handover on one PC. The phone pairs with that PC over Wi-Fi and shows the same numbers.

  • It opens on Budget vs Actual. Four totals for the year (Total Income RM11,095.00, Total Expense RM4,006.30, Total Budget RM6,660.00, Surplus / Deficit RM7,088.70) and a month-by-month table with a running cumulative surplus.
  • One window. The organisation name and a year selector sit at the top. The menus are Offering, Expenses, Budget, Import / Export and Settings.
  • The phone home screen is the same year. Income, spending, surplus and the 4 claims, RM2,179.00, still waiting to be paid.

02 Offerings

Every service, entered once.

Offering → New Activities creates the activity for a service, camp or event: its income stream, date, name and remarks. Offering → Add picks that activity and takes the givers, one row each.

  • Payment screenshots turn into rows. Drop in screenshots or PDFs, several at once, and the app makes one row per receipt and reads the amount and date off each one for you to check.
  • A gift with no name is a cash lump sum. Identity and payment method fill in by themselves for a name used before.
  • Nothing is saved until Confirm.
  • The monthly report groups by week. October shows a total of RM940.00, split by method (DuitNow RM650.00, Unspecified RM210.00, Online transfer RM80.00) and by identity, and Export PDF writes it out.

03 Budget

A budget made of codes.

Every budget line has a code, a description and an amount: MIF01 Food and drinks 400.00, MIF02 Transport 300.00, CAMP01 Camp venue 3000.00. Expense lines sit on the Budget page with the income budgets below them.

  • Codes group by prefix. MIF, PJS, OUT and CAMP each get their own group. Anything else falls into Others.
  • Add lines or import them. + Add budget line and + Add income budget add one at a time, and Import budget… brings a set in.
  • Income has a target too. Offering → Yearly shows progress to target for each income stream.

04 Claims

A claim that knows the budget.

Expenses → New takes a category, a description, an amount, who is claiming it and the receipts. As soon as you pick the category, the form shows its budget and what is left of it: for MIF01 - Food and drinks, “Budget RM400.00 · RM147.90 left” under the amount.

  • The amount types like a calculator. Digits fill in from the right.
  • Receipts go on the claim. The form has a Receipts drop zone, and the claim keeps them.

05 Payback

Paid back, and checked.

Expenses → Payment lists every claim with a Claimed or Unclaimed pill. “Still to pay back” at the top totals every unclaimed claim across all years: RM2,179.00 here.

  • Mark Paid checks the transfer. It opens the claim, and you attach the bank-transfer confirmation as the Transactional Receipt. The app checks its amount against the claim before it lets you mark the claim paid, so a mismatch is caught then, not at year end.
  • Paid claims lock. Claimed rows are greyed out with a padlock and their details can't be edited. Mark Unpaid asks first, then unlocks the claim.
  • Some claims have no transfer. A checkbox, “No transactional receipt required”, covers them.

06 Spending

See a line running out.

Expenses → Yearly sets spend against budget: Total Spend RM4,006.30, Total Budget RM6,660.00 and Total Remaining RM2,653.70. Under the totals, each budget line gets a card, grouped by its code prefix.

  • The bars change colour. A line's bar turns orange as it nears its budget and red once it passes.
  • Each card says what is left. MIF02 — Transport shows RM288.20 of RM300.00, Remaining RM11.80.

07 Android

The treasurer's numbers, in a pocket.

The Android companion shows what the PC holds: the year's income, spending and surplus, the budget, income by month and every expense claim.

  • Home is the year at a glance. Income collected in 2026 RM11,095.00 (71% of RM15,700.00), spent RM4,006.30 (60% of RM6,660.00), surplus RM7,088.70.
  • Unclaimed claims are one tap away. A card reads “4 claims · RM2,179.00” and opens Payments.
  • Four tabs and a year chip. Home, Budget, Income and Payments; the chip switches years.
  • Light, dark or system. The app follows the theme you pick.

08 Numbers

Budget and income, line by line.

The Budget tab sets every income stream and expense line against its budget, with a bar for each. The Income tab goes by month, and tapping a month opens each service, giver, method and amount.

  • Budget. The income total comes first, then each stream: Weekly Fellowship Offering RM7,540.00 of RM9,000.00, Chinese New Year tithe RM890.00 of RM2,500.00. Others reads “No budget set”.
  • Income. June 2026 totals RM2,530.00. Under it is each service, such as Fellowship night on 5 Jun 2026, with every giver, their method (DuitNow, Online transfer or Cash) and the amount.

09 Payments

Every claim, and the paying of it.

The Payments tab splits claims into Unclaimed, Claimed and All (4, 10 and 14 here). Claims are grouped by month, each with its receipt count, and the search box takes a description, a name or an amount.

  • A claim shows its receipts. Open one and you see the claim receipts and the Transfer receipt, which you attach with Camera, Photo or PDF.
  • Mark paid or Delete claim. Delete claim asks before it does anything.

10 New claim

A claim started where the receipt is.

New expense on the phone takes the same things as on the PC: category, description, amount, who is claiming it, and up to 5 receipts. Under the title, “Saved to” names the PC it goes to.

  • The camera is a receipt scanner. It finds the receipt, crops it (drag the corners to adjust) and saves it in sharp black and white.
  • The budget line updates as you type. “Budget RM400.00 · RM147.90 left · RM61.40 left after this claim” for an amount of 86.50.
  • It is saved straight to the PC. There is nothing to copy across later.

11 Pairing

Same Wi-Fi, nothing online.

On the PC, Settings → Phone → Pair phone shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone, or type the code in. From then on the phone talks to that PC and no other.

  • Only on the same Wi-Fi, and encrypted. The PC's certificate is pinned at pairing. Nothing goes to the internet.
  • It works with the PC off. Every screen shows the last synced copy straight away, along with when it last synced. Pull to refresh syncs again.
  • Background sync is optional. When it is on, the phone checks the PC about once a minute on Wi-Fi and alerts you to a new unclaimed claim.

12 Handover

Hand over a folder, not an account.

At year end, two buttons on Import / Export do the work. Export Report writes an Excel file with every offering and expense, monthly totals, and ledger sheets that list the receipt file behind each entry, plus a one-page PDF summary. Export Handover makes one zip: the year's report, every receipt, a list matching each file to its record, and the database.

  • One folder. The program, the database, the receipts vault and the backups all live in it. The database is backed up every time the app opens, and the last 10 are kept.
  • Verify Vault. In Settings, it re-checks every filed receipt's fingerprint and reports anything missing or altered.
  • No account, no subscription. Handing over means giving the next treasurer the folder. The same page also adds a new year.

13 Availability

Closed source, built for one church.

MIF Finance was built for one church's treasurer. Neither app has a public download.

  • Windows app. Closed source, no public download.
