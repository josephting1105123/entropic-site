Card Games

Web Live Open source

Dou Di Zhu, Big Two and Blackjack against bots at seven strengths, or Dou Di Zhu and Big Two online with friends. Coins are play money and there is no account. Once it has loaded, single player works offline.

Play Card Games Source on GitHub

How it works

  1. Pick a game

    Dou Di Zhu, Big Two or Blackjack, in either the American or the Malaysian Ban Luck rules. Niu Niu and Texas Hold'em are planned.

  2. Pick a table

    Choose a bot strength from Novice to Grandmaster and a stake, from a 400-chip rescue table up to a 1,000,000 pot.

  3. Play

    Play the bots on your own, or open a Dou Di Zhu or Big Two room and send friends the invite link. Bots fill any empty seat.

Details

  • Dou Di Zhu (斗地主) has three players, one landlord against two farmers. Big Two (锄大地) has four.
  • The bots only see what a player at their seat would see. A stronger bot plays better; it never sees more cards.
  • Each game keeps an Elo rating for you.
  • Coins are play money only. There are no real-money stakes, no payments and no transfers.
  • It runs in the browser, installs to the home screen as an app, and opens full screen in landscape.
  • Online rooms are for Dou Di Zhu and Big Two. The host picks the coins, the stake and how many seats are human, then shares a link or a five-character code, and friends join from any browser. Bots take over a seat if someone drops, and that player can rejoin the same hand.
  • The room server sleeps when nobody has played for a while, so the first game after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to start.
  • On a home network one computer can host rooms itself instead: run node server/server.js from the source code and everyone opens the address it prints.
