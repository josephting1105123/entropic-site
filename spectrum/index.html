It checks Spectrum so you don't have to.

Spectrum+ checks Spectrum in the background every 30 minutes. You get a notification for new announcements, assignments and materials that apply to your OCC.

Opening the app refreshes announcements and due dates within a minute, so you never wait for the next check.

Your login stays with Microsoft.

  • Sign in with your UM Microsoft account (your siswa365 address), the same way Spectrum does.
  • The password goes to Microsoft only. The app keeps just the Spectrum token, encrypted on your phone.
  • Your posts, timetable and deadlines stay on your phone. The app talks to Spectrum and nothing else.
  • Your OCC for each course comes from your MAYA timetable, cross-checked with your Spectrum group. When a post doesn't say which OCC, it's shown and labelled instead of hidden.

Need the full Spectrum page? It's one tap away.

Every item has "Open in Spectrum". It opens the real page inside the app, restyled for your phone, with sections for other occurrences hidden. Quizzes and attendance happen there, on Spectrum itself.
